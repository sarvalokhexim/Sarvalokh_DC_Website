Standard Export Packing Specifications

  • Multi-layer Protective Bags: Export cargo packed in 25 kg Multi-layer Kraft Paper Bags or HDPE/PP woven bags with food-grade inner LDPE liner to preserve aroma and prevent moisture ingress.
  • Safe Warehouse Storage: Stored in clean, well-ventilated, dry, and infestation-free warehouses prior to container stuffing.
  • FCL stuffing Capacity: A 20ft container carries ~12 to 13 Metric Tons. A 40ft High Cube container carries ~25 Metric Tons.
